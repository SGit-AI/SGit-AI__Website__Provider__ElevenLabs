# The briefs this site was built from

Raw source, published in full, in the shape the rest of the `*.sgit.ai` network uses: the
markdown as it arrived, with its licence stamp, next to a page on the site that says what was
done with it. **The reviewed, decision-by-decision version is at
[/briefs/](https://elevenlabs.providers.sgit.ai/briefs/)** — this folder is the evidence behind it.

| Folder | What it is | Date | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| `2026-09-07__pack__providers-site/` | Six documents: the audit of the live site at v0.1.1, two axes and eleven decisions, an eight-step plan, twelve acceptance tests, eight open questions | 7 Sep 2026 | **Mostly implemented at v0.2.0.** Three of its audit findings were wrong and one proposed capability row was rejected — see the site page |
| `2026-09-07__brief__video-pipeline/` | A working, measured video pipeline and the first-run guide for its unrun ElevenLabs path | 7 Sep 2026 | **Processed, not executed.** Published as a plan with its numbers badged; no video has been made |

The pack that produced the site's first release — the 5 September brief for
`elevenlabs.providers.sgit.ai` v1 — is not in this folder; its content is the site.

## What is deliberately not published here

Both packs were derived from a wider corpus. Nine source briefs came with the September pack and
are **not** republished, because they are about the estate rather than about this provider, and a
site whose argument is that it is narrower than the vendor's own environment does not get to
publish everything it was sent. Named so the omission is visible:

| Source brief | Why it is not here |
|---|---|
| `v0.33.65 strategy · platforms is the right shelf and the wrong spine` | The 5 September brief. **Its content is the site itself.** One of its claims — the signed-URL rule — is corrected on the report, per product |
| `v0.33.66 dev · specification for the providers pack` | About the family and a sibling migration, not about this provider |
| `v0.33.66 strategy · a playground is an explorer artefact` | The reasoning behind these sites; its two operative rules (a failure ratio, and an exit) are quoted on the site page |
| `v0.33.66 dev · the clone ships and cannot start from inside the template` | The video vault's release mechanism. Estate platform work |
| `v0.33.66 dev · two linked vault lanes`, `arch · the append lane sits outside the commit DAG`, `dev · the reply address travels inside the first message` | Vault platform architecture. Relevant only to a tier-three tool, which does not exist here |
| `v0.33.66 strategy · the game primitives are latent in nine designs` | A different site entirely |
| `v0.33.66 index · 2026-09-07` | The day index for the whole corpus |

If you want one of them, it is the project lead's to release, not this site's.

## The rule these obey

Publishing the brief next to the thing it commissioned is the same discipline as the rest of this
site: **the claim and its evidence travel together.** A site that says what it was asked to build
can be checked against it; one that only shows the result cannot.
