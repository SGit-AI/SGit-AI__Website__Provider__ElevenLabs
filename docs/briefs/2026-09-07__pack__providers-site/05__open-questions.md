# 05: Open Questions

**Do not decide these alone.** Each is either the project lead's, or needs a party outside this site.

---

| # | Question | Why it is open |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Is the grant applied for, and when is it disclosed?** | The disclosures page promises that any programme is recorded there first. An application is the first moment that promise is testable, and the ordering is the point. **Blocking on the project lead** |
| 2 | Does a speech counterpart to the listen call get built? | It is the natural first integration for this provider, it fits the existing bridge, and it is a platform decision rather than a site one |
| 3 | Which workloads are named in "what we use it for"? | Only the project lead knows which are real and which are intended, and the section is worthless if it contains the second kind |
| 4 | Does tier three ship for this provider, or stay a specification? | It needs the platform's key custody work, and the honest label is available today either way |
| 5 | How much of the video toolchain is this provider's job? | An April decision chose a browser stack for zero marginal cost; using a hosted voice reverses it. **Both are probably right for different outputs** and nobody has drawn the line |
| 6 | Is a measured latency distribution worth publishing? | Three samples are not a distribution and the site says so. A real one is the kind of number only a third party publishes, and it costs credits to produce |
| 7 | Who owns the links between this site and its siblings? | Open since 20 August: several site agents, one composition, no named owner |
| 8 | Does the site publish our own key's scope? | It would be the most concrete possible statement about pattern zero with a ceiling, and it is an operational disclosure |

## Two Notes For Whoever Answers Them

**Question 5 is the one that will be answered by drifting if nobody answers it deliberately.** The April brief chose a local stack explicitly for zero marginal cost per video; a hosted voice brings quality, languages and alignment and brings a key, a per-character cost and a demonstration that stops working when downloaded. **Write the decision down whichever way it goes.**

**Question 1 has a deadline that is not obvious.** The disclosures page currently reads that there are no relationships, checked on a date. **That sentence becomes false the moment an application is approved**, and the page's own commitment is to have recorded it before then, not after.
